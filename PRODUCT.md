# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who want to access DeFi strategies without selecting and monitoring every asset or protocol themselves, including people new to on-chain investing and existing Web3 wallet users.

## Product purpose

Banda makes it simpler to own and manage a DeFi portfolio through managed investment baskets. Users deposit USDG and choose a basket; they should not need to bridge individual assets. Where an asset is not native to Robinhood Chain, Banda may only use an issuer- or market-maker-provided canonical representation with a verified USDG buy/sell route, price source, and redemption path. Banda does not issue wrappers or custody cross-chain reserves in the MVP.

Lead with ease of access, understandable strategy choices, and a single portfolio view. Privy is the planned login and user-wallet layer: new users can start with familiar login methods and an embedded/smart wallet, while Web3 users can connect an external wallet. The user's wallet owns the basket NFT; the ERC-6551 basket account holds the portfolio and is a distinct, restricted contract.

Portfolio transfer and NFT/account architecture support that experience; they are not the primary selling point. Avoid claims of guaranteed returns, universal eligibility, unrestricted access, or live asset availability that has not been verified. The landing page leads to a clearly labelled local demonstration.

## Brand personality

Precise, quiet, established. A printed portfolio certificate on a daylight-lit desk, examined carefully by an investor. Light mode is primary.

## Anti-references

No decorative crypto visuals, gradients, glow, fake trust strips, vanity metrics, default feature-card grids, or rounded surfaces.

## Design principles

Show the portfolio itself. Distinguish example data from live data. Explain ownership visually. Keep future capabilities explicitly separate from initial scope. Use the accepted warm-paper and spice-green palette.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus, readable contrast, mobile reflow, and reduced-motion final states as required by the frontend brief.
