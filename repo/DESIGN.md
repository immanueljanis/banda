# Banda visual system

This document records the frontend visual baseline; current product decisions are in the [Open House brief](../openhouse/README.md). Warm paper, warm ink, deep spice green, gold for commodities, and muted sage for yield. Manrope grotesk with Geist Mono for all figures. Headlines capped at 56px. One-pixel rules, squared certificates, no shadows. The hero certificate and account tree are the imagery. Motion is restricted to data and the once-only position consolidation. Dark mode uses separately defined readable surface and foreground tokens.
