# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who want to access DeFi strategies without selecting and monitoring every asset or protocol themselves, including people new to on-chain investing.

## Product Purpose

Banda makes it simpler to own and manage a DeFi portfolio through managed investment baskets. Lead with ease of access, understandable strategy choices and a single portfolio view. Portfolio transfer and NFT/account architecture support that experience; they are not the primary selling point. Avoid claims of guaranteed returns, universal eligibility or unrestricted access. The landing page leads to a clearly labelled local demonstration. The [Open House brief](../openhouse/README.md) is the source of current product decisions.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, established. A printed portfolio certificate on a daylight-lit desk, examined carefully by an investor. Light mode is primary.

## Anti-references

No decorative crypto visuals, gradients, glow, fake trust strips, vanity metrics, default feature-card grids, or rounded surfaces.

## Design Principles

Show the portfolio itself. Distinguish example data from live data. Explain ownership visually. Keep future capabilities explicitly separate from initial scope. Use the accepted warm-paper and spice-green palette.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus, readable contrast, mobile reflow, and reduced-motion final states as required by the frontend brief.
