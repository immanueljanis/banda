# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Investors who want diversified on-chain exposure without selecting and monitoring every asset or protocol themselves. Banda must work for both Web2 users entering through Privy and experienced Web3 users connecting an external wallet.

## Product Purpose

Banda makes a managed DeFi portfolio easier to understand and operate. Users compare transparent Basket mandates, deposit USDG, own an ERC-721 Basket backed by an ERC-6551 account, monitor its position, and redeem partially or fully. Success means the live contract state, costs, risks, provenance, and transaction lifecycle remain understandable without overstating liquidity, performance, or production readiness.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and candid. The interface should feel like a considered investment instrument: approachable enough for a first-time on-chain investor, rigorous enough for a user who verifies every address and transaction.

## Anti-references

Avoid casino-like crypto dashboards, neon trading terminals, speculative yield hype, opaque performance claims, generic SaaS card grids, and interfaces that disguise testnet fixtures or synthetic data as investable mainnet assets.

## Design Principles

1. Show provenance and state, not unsupported certainty.
2. Keep the primary path from Basket selection to redemption legible.
3. Make contract, network, fee, and transaction consequences visible before action.
4. Distinguish live state, simulation, historical data, and unavailable data explicitly.
5. Preserve recovery paths for rejected signatures, RPC failures, stale prices, and paused contracts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, semantic status messaging, and reduced-motion support. Never encode transaction or risk state by color alone, and keep monetary figures readable with tabular numerals and explicit units.
