# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

On-chain investors evaluating a diversified portfolio that can move as one object, at any investment size.

## Product Purpose

Banda is an Arbitrum index provider. The landing page explains basket ownership and leads to a clearly labelled local demonstration. PRD.md and FE-PROMPT.md in the parent directory are the source of truth.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, established. A printed portfolio certificate on a daylight-lit desk, examined carefully by an investor. Light mode is primary.

## Anti-references

No decorative crypto visuals, gradients, glow, fake trust strips, vanity metrics, default feature-card grids, or rounded surfaces.

## Design Principles

Show the portfolio itself. Distinguish example data from live data. Explain ownership visually. Keep future capabilities explicitly separate from initial scope. Use the accepted warm-paper and spice-green palette.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus, readable contrast, mobile reflow, and reduced-motion final states as required by the frontend brief.
